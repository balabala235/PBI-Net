PyTorch profiler counts supported multiply-add/linear/convolution operators (one multiply-add = two FLOPs). FFT cost is added separately using 2.5*N*log2(N) real operations per real FFT. The one CFPT PTM Conv2d rejected by torch.profiler because of padding='same' is added analytically from its observed tensor geometry. Remaining elementwise and unsupported operators are not included, so approx_total_flops is an implementation-consistent approximation/lower bound, not an exact hardware instruction count.
